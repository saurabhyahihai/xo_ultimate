Privacy Policy for XO Ultimate

Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to XO Ultimate ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use the XO Ultimate mobile application (the "App").

The app does not collect or store any user data for itself, and is completely offline. Instead, all data collection is done securely by the Google AdMob SDK, and it is used exclusively for advertisement purposes.

1. Information We Collect

XO Ultimate is designed to be a simple, privacy-friendly Tic-Tac-Toe game. We collect minimal information to ensure the proper functioning of the App and to display advertisements.

1.1 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, certain information is collected automatically by the ad network (Google AdMob) we use to display advertisements. This may include:

  • Device Information (such as device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers like advertising IDs)
  • Usage Data (such as interaction with ads)
  • Network Information (such as IP address to infer general location for ad targeting)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To Display Advertisements: To provide relevant ads via Google AdMob.
  • To Manage Consent: For users in applicable regions (such as the EEA/UK), we use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to gather and manage your privacy choices and ad personalization settings.
  • To Improve the App: To understand how users interact with the App and fix any technical issues.

3. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob: Used for displaying advertisements in the App. You can learn more about Google's privacy practices by visiting their Privacy Policy.
  • Expo: Used as the framework to build the App.

4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us with your information and strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage, is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. However, we do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.
