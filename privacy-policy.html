Privacy Policy for XO

Effective Date: October 7, 2026

Welcome to XO, an advanced strategic Tic-Tac-Toe application. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and protected when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in keeping your data as private as possible. The XO app does not require you to create an account, nor do we directly collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, address, or phone number.

  • Local Game Data: All your gameplay statistics, Brain Score ratings, settings (like theme and sound preferences), and daily streak information are stored locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to our servers.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

To keep the app free, we use third-party advertising services, specifically Unity Ads, to display simulated rewarded advertisements.

These third-party networks may automatically collect non-personal information to serve relevant ads. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA).
  • IP addresses and general geographic location.
  • App usage data and interaction metrics.

For more information on how Unity collects and processes your data, please review the Unity Privacy Policy.

3. Your Privacy Choices (GDPR & CCPA)

We respect your right to privacy and comply with global privacy regulations, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA).

  • Consent on Launch: Upon launching the app for the first time, you will be presented with a Privacy & Ads consent modal.
  • Opt-in/Opt-out: You have the absolute right to choose whether you wish to see personalized ads or non-personalized ads.
  • Managing Preferences: You can update your consent choices at any time directly from the in-game Settings menu.

We ensure that our advertising SDKs are strictly initialized only after your consent preferences have been safely recorded.

4. Children's Privacy

XO is a general audience game. However, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. Furthermore, we explicitly configure our ad networks to avoid collecting data from children (COPPA compliance). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or regulatory requirements. Any changes will be posted within this document, and the "Effective Date" will be updated accordingly.
